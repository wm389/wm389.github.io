Bar Inventory Tracker

I built this in Excel from the paper liquor count sheet at the banquet bar where I work. For each event, I enter the guest count and the start, addition, and closing counts. It then shows how much we used, what it cost, our pour cost %, and what we need to reorder. It also breaks usage down by category and by guest, and a planner tab uses that to estimate what to stock for a bigger or smaller event.

Financial Architecture & Methodology

  • Income Smoothing: Consolidates variable cash tips and direct-deposit hourly wages into annualized and rolling 4-week averages to stabilize discretionary spending.
  • Withholding Reserve: Built-in tax set-aside formulas allocate estimated federal/state liabilities before calculating disposable income.
  • Zero-Based Allocation: Organizes monthly obligations into fixed overhead, essential living expenses, and automated transfers toward liquid emergency savings.
