Cost Accounting & Inventory Control Excel / Operations

Bar Inventory Tracker

Built in Excel to replace the manual paper liquor count sheet at the banquet bar. For each event, entering the guest count alongside start, addition, and closing counts dynamically calculates total volume used, item cost, pour cost percentage, and required reorder quantities. Breaks down usage by category and per guest, featuring a dedicated planner tab that forecasts exact stock requirements for larger or smaller event headcounts.

Key Capabilities:

  • Pour cost % tracking and automated reorder threshold triggers
  • Category and per-guest liquor consumption variance analytics
  • Event stock planning model based on variable guest counts
